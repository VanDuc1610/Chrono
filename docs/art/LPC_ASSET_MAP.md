# CHRONO – LPC Asset Map (Chapters 0–9)

**Target standard:** Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) 32 px tiles, 3/4 perspective (walls with height, cliffs, stairs), characters ~1.5 tiles tall in `64x64` frames.  
**Licence:** CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0 (all sheets require author credits in `CREDITS.md` via T-89).  
**Sources:**

- Tiles & Props: `LiberatedPixelCup/RPG_Maker_MZ_LPC_Starter_Kit` (`LPC/img/tilesets/LPC/` and `Final Attributions/Individual Assets/`).
- NPC Outfits: `Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator` (`body`, `hair`, `torso`, `legs`, `feet`, `belt`, `cape`, `arms`, `weapon`, `hat`).
- **Neon Skin (`T-86`) Boundary:** Chapters 0–5 use **full LPC tilesets with light neon accents** (25%–70% outline/glow); Chapters 6–9 shift to **full cosmic neon void** (`level = 1.0`), using LPC mainly for silhouettes, y-sorted prop anchors, and NPC/boss bases.
