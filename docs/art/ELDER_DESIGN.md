# ELDER CHRONOGRAPHER – CHARACTER DESIGN SPECIFICATION

**Character Name:** Elder Chronographer  
**Role:** Sanctuary Guardian, Lore Guide & Quest Giver (Prologue NPC)  
**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Project:** CHRONO – THE FRACTURED DIVINITY  
**Governing Rules:** [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) and [.agents/rules/chrono-art.md](../../.agents/rules/chrono-art.md)

## 1. Narrative Context & Visual Pillar

The Elder Chronographer is a venerable survivor of the Monastic Order of the Divided Hour. Having spent decades recording cosmic temporal alignments within the mountain sanctuary, he now safeguards the Altar of Moments amidst the ruins of the Cataclysm.

- **Atmosphere:** Ancient, scholarly, weathered, dignified, ethereal.
- **Silhouette:** Distinct monastic cowl/hood, flowing white-silver beard, layered scholar robes, holding a brass astrolabe staff in his left hand.

---

## 2. Core Physical Traits

- **Age & Appearance:** Appears approximately 70 years old. Venerable, weathered, dignified posture, slightly stooped scholarly shoulders.
- **Hair & Beard:** Flowing snow-white hair emerging from hood; long trimmed silver beard reaching chest.
- **Eyes:** Deep-set pale amber-gold eyes (`#d19e34`) indicating lifelong attunement to temporal energies.
- **Skin:** Aged weathered parchment tone (`#c9b097`, shadow `#9e836b`), fine etched wrinkle lines.

---

## 3. Costume & Regalia

### 3.1 The Order's Vestments
- **Cowl & Mantle:** Deep midnight-indigo hood and mantle (`#161a29`, highlight `#28324f`) with aged bronze trim along the hem.
- **Tunic / Robe:** Heavy slate-gray monastic cassock (`#262d3d`) bound by a thick leather waist girdle.
- **Stole:** Embroidered liturgical stole draped over both shoulders depicting the 12 celestial hours in faded gold thread (`#8c6628`).
- **Boots:** Soft dark leather scholar slippers (`#221d18`).

### 3.2 Signature Objects
1. **The Astrolabe Staff:**
   - Slender petrified oak staff held in the left hand.
   - Tipped with an ornate rotating brass astrolabe ring framing a suspended pulsing amber chrono-shard (`#ffd666`).
2. **Scroll Satchel:**
   - Weathered leather pouch mounted at right hip filled with rolled parchment celestial maps.
