## 2. Non-Negotiable Core Physical Features

- **Age & Build:** Adult male, approximately 26 years old. Slender-athletic, practical explorer physique. Mature masculine facial proportions.
- **Hairstyle (Permanent Identity Lock):**
  - Short, compact, naturally curly men's haircut (`#3b291d`, `#22160f`, `#594132`).
  - Consists of clearly distinguishable, tightly coiled, springy curls.
  - Overall hair silhouette must remain rounded, compact, and slightly elevated.
  - Curls must grow upward and outward rather than hanging down.
  - The entire head must retain visible curly hair coverage, including the sides and back.
  - **STRICT PROHIBITIONS – Never interpret Peter's hairstyle as:**
    - Straight messy hair.
    - Wavy hair.
    - Loose flowing curls.
    - Long curly fringe.
    - Drooping bangs.
    - Shaggy or layered hair.
    - Undercut or shaved sides.
    - Long hair, shoulder-length hair, or ponytail.
  - The hairstyle is a permanent character identity feature and cannot be changed without explicit approval from the project owner.
- **Facial Features:** Focused, restrained, contemplative expression. Muted gray-blue eyes (`#607d9c`). Defined jawline with faint shadow/stubble.
- **Skin Tone:** Fair to neutral weathered skin (`#dab9a0`, shadow `#b28e76`).

## 3. Costume & Equipment Details (From Approved Concept)

### 3.1 The Charcoal-Blue Travel Coat
- **Color:** Deep charcoal-blue / dark slate-navy (`#263248`, shadow `#161e2e`, highlight `#3d4f70`).
- **Accents:** Restrained dark purple-slate interior lining and trim accents (`#281e36`, `#3a2a4f`).
- **Length:** Mid-calf duster cut.
- **Collar:** High structured stand-up collar flaring slightly at the throat.
- **Chest Clasps:** Four distinct horizontal pairs of aged-silver bar-and-toggle clasps (`#8c96a8`) running from the collar down to the waist.
- **Belt:** Dark leather waist belt with a rectangular aged-silver buckle.

### 3.2 Undergarments & Practical Layering
- **Tunic:** Dark charcoal vertically ribbed travel shirt (`#12151d`) visible at the throat.
- **Trousers:** Durable slate-black traveler's trousers (`#181c24`).
- **Boots:** Mid-calf dark leather field/riding boots (`#2e2219`) with reinforced turnover cuffs.

### 3.3 Signature Objects & Accessories
1. **The Broken Blade (Primary Weapon):**
   - Held in the right hand in all baseline poses.
   - Hilt: Spherical aged-silver pommel, dark wire/leather wrapped grip, and straight cruciform silver crossguard.
   - Blade: Ancient steel broadsword broken off midway along its length with an angled, jagged fracture point.
   - Aura: Luminous cerulean chronal glow (`#00c8ff`, `#a6f0ff`) emanating along the fuller and fractured edge.
2. **The Stopped Watch:**
   - Ornate silver pocket watch suspended by an aged silver chain from the waist belt / coat pocket.
   - Round white watch face with frozen filigree hands.
3. **The Torn Notebook:**
   - Thick, leather-bound journal mounted to the diagonal shoulder strap at the left hip.
   - Cream parchment page edges visible between weathered dark leather covers.
4. **The Diagonal Shoulder Strap:**
   - Weathered dark-brown oiled leather strap (`#453123`) crossing diagonally from the right shoulder down to the left hip.
   - Secured with a square aged-silver buckle on the chest.

## 5. Proportions & Translation to Top-Down 16-Bit Sprites

The approved concept sheet is an **illustrative design turnaround (~7 heads tall)** and is **NOT a directly usable in-game sprite**.

### In-Game Top-Down Sprite Translation Rules:
1. **Resolution & Canvas:** $32 \times 32\text{ px}$ per animation frame.
2. **Stylized Proportion:** 1:2.8 to 1:3 head-to-body ratio (chibi-stylized realism standard for 16-bit top-down RPGs).
3. **Footprint Collider:** $18 \times 14\text{ px}$ collision box at center bottom `(7, 16)`.
4. **Mandatory Silhouette Features to Preserve in 32x32:**
   - Rounded compact curly hair dome (dark brown, curly texture pixel cluster).
   - High coat collar framing the head.
   - Visible diagonal leather strap crossing chest/back.
   - Hip-mounted notebook silhouette on left.
   - Broken Blade with cerulean glow in right hand.
   - Muted gray-blue eye pixels in down-facing frames.
