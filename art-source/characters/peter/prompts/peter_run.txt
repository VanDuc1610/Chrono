Top-Down 16-Bit Sprite Specification: Peter - Run Animation Sheet

Target Format:
- Grid: 32x32 pixels per frame, 6 frames per direction, 4 cardinal directions:
  - Row 0: Down-facing (sprinting towards camera)
  - Row 1: Up-facing (sprinting away from camera)
  - Row 2: Left-facing (sprinting profile left)
  - Row 3: Right-facing (sprinting profile right)
- Total dimensions: 192x128 pixels (6x4 frames).
- Background: 100% transparent (8-bit alpha channel).
- Footprint collider: 18x14 pixels at bottom center (7, 16).

Character Consistency Requirements (from approved concept peter_concept_v1.png):
- Hair (Permanent Identity Lock): Short, compact, naturally curly dark-brown hair (#3b291d) with clearly distinguishable, tightly coiled, springy curls. Silhouette is rounded, compact, and slightly elevated; curls grow upward/outward rather than hanging down. Complete curly coverage across entire head, sides, and back. NO straight, wavy, loose curls, drooping bangs, shaggy hair, or undercut/shaved sides.
- Muted gray-blue eyes (#607d9c) visible on down-facing frames.
- High structured coat collar framing the head.
- Asymmetrical charcoal-blue travel coat (#263248) flaring back with dynamic motion, showing restrained purple lining (#281e36).
- Diagonal dark-brown leather shoulder strap (#453123) from right shoulder to left hip.
- Left hip torn notebook held close to body during sprint.
- Right hand holding Broken Blade with luminous cerulean fracture glow (#00c8ff).
- Stopped watch suspended from waist belt.
- Dark trousers and leather boots with pronounced dynamic strides and forward lean.

Motion:
- 6-frame fast tactical sprint cycle per direction:
  - High knee drive, deliberate forward body tilt (2-pixel lean).
  - Rapid alternating arm pump and blade trailing.
  - Coat tails billowing outward behind the runner.
- Frame rate: 12 FPS loop.
