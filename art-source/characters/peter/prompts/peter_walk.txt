================================================================================
GRID & TARGET FORMAT:
================================================================================
- Dimensions: 192x128 pixels total sprite sheet (6 columns x 4 rows)
- Frame Size: 32x32 pixels per frame
- Rows (4 Cardinal Directions):
  - Row 0 (Y=0..31): Down-facing walk cycle (6 frames)
  - Row 1 (Y=32..63): Up-facing walk cycle (6 frames)
  - Row 2 (Y=64..95): Left-facing walk cycle (6 frames)
  - Row 3 (Y=96..127): Right-facing walk cycle (6 frames)
- Background: 100% transparent (8-bit alpha channel)
- Collision Footprint: 18x14 pixels at center-bottom (7, 16)
- Animation Speed: 9 FPS loop

STRIDE CYCLE VERIFICATION (6 FRAMES PER ROW):
================================================================================
- Frame 0: Left foot contact / forward plant
- Frame 1: Left foot passing / body rises 1px
- Frame 2: Right foot reach / apex extension
- Frame 3: Right foot contact / forward plant
- Frame 4: Right foot passing / body rises 1px
- Frame 5: Left foot reach / return to contact
- Ground plane contact maintained consistently across all frames.
- Hair silhouette volume and curl definition must not distort or flatten during walking.
