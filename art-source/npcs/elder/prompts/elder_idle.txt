GRID & TARGET FORMAT:
================================================================================
- Dimensions: 128x128 pixels total sprite sheet (4 columns x 4 rows)
- Frame Size: 32x32 pixels per frame
- Rows (4 Cardinal Directions):
  - Row 0 (Y=0..31): Down-facing idle (4 breathing frames)
  - Row 1 (Y=32..63): Up-facing idle (4 breathing frames)
  - Row 2 (Y=64..95): Left-facing idle (4 breathing frames)
  - Row 3 (Y=96..127): Right-facing idle (4 breathing frames)
- Background: 100% transparent (8-bit alpha channel)
- Collision Footprint: 18x14 pixels at center-bottom (7, 16)
- Animation Speed: 4 FPS loop
